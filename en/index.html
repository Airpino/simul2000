The program

“A sort of statistics video game”, as a colleague put it on seeing it.

Simul2000 was born in the mid-1990s, refined in 2000 and last modified in 2003. It is not meant to replace statistical software for research: it favours graphics, to show the individual simulated samples and their estimates dynamically, compared with the true values of the parameters.

This version rebuilds the modules of the original for the browser, with the same teaching ideas, a few extensions and a module planned at the time but never finished: the comparison between the mean and the maximum likelihood estimator.

How to use it

  • Each module opens on a settings screen: distribution or model, size of the simulation, seed of the generator.
  • Then the simulation is started and its speed adjusted, or it is completed in one go.
  • Each page works on its own, even offline, and adapts to computers, tablets and phones.
  • Every module offers colours suited to people who find it hard to tell red from green.
