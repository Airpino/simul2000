Presentation

Simul2000: an interactive teaching program on statistical simulation.

In recent years I have developed an interactive teaching program to illustrate some aspects of the use of simulation techniques in statistics.

The software was developed essentially in Visual Basic (versions 1, 3 and 6), so as to favour the graphics; it is in no way meant to replace the functions of ordinary mathematical and statistical software for research, but only to bring out some aspects that are useful for teaching.

WinHelp topic 6O_F0UE

Univariate simulations

Generation of pseudo-random numbers from several univariate distributions chosen by the user, among them the uniform, the normal, the normal of order p (exponential power), the gamma, the beta, etc. The user can choose how many numbers to generate from the chosen distribution. Several options are implemented for the progressive display of the charts and of the numerical output; comparison between simulated and theoretical distribution, empirical and theoretical moments, etc.

WinHelp topic 624.FCE

Simulated distribution of arithmetic means

Simulated distribution of the arithmetic means of m samples of n pseudo-random numbers generated from univariate distributions chosen by the user. Both the sample size n and the number of samples m can be chosen. Several options are implemented for the progressive display of the charts (for the individual samples and for the distribution of the means) and of the numerical output.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI82

Integration with the Monte Carlo method

Graphical display of Monte Carlo integration, with the elementary hit or miss technique. The function to integrate and the number of points on which the estimate of the integral is based can be chosen.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI83

Methods for generating pseudo-random numbers

Dynamic illustration of the generation of pseudo-random numbers by inverting the distribution function.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI84

Linear and nonlinear regression models

Simulation of univariate regression models, linear and nonlinear. Choice of several parameters for the systematic and for the random component. Display of the individual samples, of the true and simulated relationships, and of the joint distribution of the simulated estimates of the model parameters.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI85

Generalised linear models (GLMs)

Simulation of univariate generalised linear models (GLMs), with canonical links and binomial, Poisson and gamma errors. Choice of several parameters. Display of the individual samples, of the true and simulated relationships, and of the joint distribution of the simulated estimates of the model parameters.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI86

ARMA models

Simulation of series from ARMA(p,q) models. Choice of the p+q parameters; display of the whole series and of a window on a stretch of the series.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI87

Simulation of bivariate distributions

Generation of pairs of pseudo-random numbers from some bivariate distributions chosen by the user. The user can choose how many pairs to generate from the chosen distribution; they are displayed dynamically.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI88

Archive on simulations

Archive of further material on simulations.

WinHelp topic 1YRJI89

M. Chiodi’s home page

Opens the web browser to connect to my home page. Note: the program does not check the computer’s internet connection.

WinHelp topic A6REBS

Choice of m

Choice of m, the number of pseudo-random values making up the simulated distribution.

WinHelp topic 1PCL0_A

Interval between successive histograms

Choice of mparz, the display interval between successive histograms; it lets you watch the simulated distribution take shape. A small number slows the run down; in any case the program forces mparz to be a divisor of m.

WinHelp topic XDGKBI

Choice of n, size of the individual samples

Choice of n, the size of the individual samples generated (this gives a sample of m pseudo-random arithmetic means, making up the simulated sampling distribution of the mean).

WinHelp topic 1PCL0_B

Type of histogram scale

With the box ticked, the scale of the successive histograms changes so that the chart always takes up the same area; with the box unticked, the scale stays the same in the successive charts, so the area taken up by the chart grows during the simulation.

WinHelp topic 5N58H3J

Single-generation panel

Tick the box to display a panel for each single generation. It slows the simulation down enormously, but shows the individual pseudo-random values graphically (or the individual samples with their means, when simulating distributions of arithmetic means).

WinHelp topic ILJ4XN

Display of the individual values

Tick the box to display a window with the individual simulated values (the output may be very long and weigh on system resources).

WinHelp topic 1MV_EOF

Choice of the univariate distribution

Choice of the univariate distribution from which the pseudo-random numbers are generated. Some distributions are in standardised form; for others the true values of the parameters will be chosen next.

WinHelp topic 21M6B1

Choice of the integrand

Choice of the integrand f(x). The functions are in standardised form, i.e. 0<x<1 and 0<f(x)<1.

WinHelp topic 1525IFN

Choice of the bivariate distribution

Choice of the bivariate distribution from which the pseudo-random numbers are generated. Some distributions are in standardised form; for others the true values of the parameters will be chosen next.

WinHelp topic 4PKVO7K

Generator of uniform pseudo-random numbers

In this panel you choose the generator of the uniform pseudo-random numbers used as the basis of all the simulations. Pressing the update button changes the initial seed a0 used for generators 1 and 2, which are multiplicative congruential generators; generator 3 is the built-in Visual Basic one. To repeat the same simulation, use the same value of a0 (with generators 1 or 2).
