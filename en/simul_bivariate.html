Sample statistics

 simulatedtheoretical

The regression lines are estimated on the generated pairs: the one of y on x passes through the means with slope Cov(x, y)/Var(x), the one of x on y with Var(y)/Cov(x, y). The ellipses of the normals enclose the theoretical probability p; next to them is the fraction of points falling inside. Numbers are generated in the browser with a multiplicative congruential generator.
