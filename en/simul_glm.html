Maximum likelihood estimates by Fisher scoring (iteratively reweighted least squares). The ellipses of the second window and the curves on the histograms of the b use the asymptotic normal distribution of the estimates, with covariance matrix φ(X′WX)⁻¹ evaluated at the true parameter values. For the gamma the link is logarithmic and the shape parameter ν is estimated by maximum likelihood, by solving log ν − ψ(ν) = d̄, where d̄ is the mean of −log(yᵢ/μ̂ᵢ) + (yᵢ − μ̂ᵢ)/μ̂ᵢ. Numbers are generated in the browser with a multiplicative congruential generator.
