Arithmetic mean and maximum likelihood

From each sample the location parameter is estimated in two ways, with the arithmetic mean and with the maximum likelihood estimator, and the two sampling distributions are compared.

Asymptotic variances: σ²/n for the arithmetic mean, 1/(n·I) for the maximum likelihood estimator, where I is the Fisher information of a single observation about θ. The asymptotic ratio of the two variances is the asymptotic relative efficiency, σ²·I. The scale is known in all cases: only θ is estimated. Numbers are generated in the browser with a multiplicative congruential generator.
