Histogram of the means

The theoretical values are the exact ones for the mean of n independent observations: mean μ, variance σ²/n, skewness γ₁/√n, kurtosis 3 + (β₂ − 3)/n. The confidence interval of each sample is x̄ ± z·s/√n, with the normal quantile and the sample standard deviation s: for small n the actual coverage stays below the nominal one. Numbers are generated in the browser with a multiplicative congruential generator.
