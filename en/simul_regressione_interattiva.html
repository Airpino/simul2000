Background

Interactive linear regression

Points are added with the mouse or a finger and moved around: the least-squares line, the confidence band for the line and the band for individual values update as the point moves.

ŷ = a + b·x x = –; y = –
  • click on an empty spot: adds a point
  • drag: moves a point
  • double-click or right-click: deletes a point (on a phone, press and hold)

Options

Least-squares line

Confidence band for the line: ŷ(x) ± t·s·√(1/n + (x − x̄)²/Sxx); band for individual values: ŷ(x) ± t·s·√(1 + 1/n + (x − x̄)²/Sxx), with t the 1 − α/2 quantile of Student’s t with n − 2 degrees of freedom and s² = Σ(yᵢ − ŷᵢ)²/(n − 2). The bands widen moving away from x̄: that is why a point far from the mean of the x moves the line much more than one close to it.
